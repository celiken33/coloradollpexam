Why the license changes the earning equation

A paralegal’s billing ceiling is set by supervision — their work is billed under an attorney. An LLP’s ceiling is set by their own book: the license authorizes independent advice, signed filings, and court appearances across the family-law scope (What can an LLP do?). That means an LLP can bill for legal services directly — inside a firm at an LLP rate, or in an LLP-owned practice serving the enormous unrepresented market at rates families can actually pay.

The demand side is not speculative: three of four Colorado domestic-relations parties navigate court with no professional at all. The Colorado Supreme Court created this license precisely to move that number.
