LLP vs. paralegal — same word, different license

One works under a supervising attorney. The other holds a Colorado Supreme Court license to advise, file, and appear in court. Here is the whole difference.

Same word, different license. “Paralegal” is a job; “Licensed Legal Paraprofessional” is a license issued by the Colorado Supreme Court. The difference decides who may advise clients, sign filings, and stand up in court.

Why the license exists

In FY2022, roughly 74% of Colorado domestic-relations parties had no lawyer (OARC 2025 Annual Report). The Colorado Supreme Court built the LLP license so experienced legal professionals could serve those families at accessible rates — with real authority and real accountability under the LLP Rules of Professional Conduct.
