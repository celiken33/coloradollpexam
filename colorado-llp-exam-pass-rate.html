What those numbers actually tell you

First-timers who prepare pass. An 87% first-time rate is a prepared-cohort number — most candidates are working paralegals who took the runway seriously. The repeat rate falling to ~60% is the warning: the second climb is statistically harder, which is why retakers need a different plan, not the same one again (that’s the entire premise of THE SECOND ASCENT).

The cut is around 68% — on 135 multiple-choice questions plus two essays, that leaves you roughly 43 questions of room.
