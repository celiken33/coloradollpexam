Quick Answers

The LLP license, in brief.

What is a Licensed Legal Paraprofessional?

A new license created by the Colorado Supreme Court allowing qualified paraprofessionals to represent clients in less-complex domestic relations matters — dissolution, parentage, and allocation of parental responsibilities — within the scope defined by C.R.C.P. 207.1.

What's actually on the exam?

Two multiple-choice sections (family law and LLP ethics) plus essays, testing the published LLP Core Competencies. The practice bank mirrors that structure at 3× volume: 270 family-law MC, 135 ethics MC, and 6 essays.

Am I eligible to sit in November?

Eligibility runs through the Office of Attorney Regulation Counsel — education plus documented paralegal experience pathways. Check the official application instructions and file by the deadline.
